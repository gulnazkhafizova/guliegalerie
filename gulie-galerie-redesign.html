Paintings made to be lived with

Gulnaz Khafizova paints landscapes, still life and portraits in acrylic, watercolor and oil — each piece built slowly, by hand, for a home rather than a wall label.

View the gallery Commission a piece

"Each piece is a reflection of my inner world, and an invitation for you to see it in your own way."

I'm Gulnaz — a painter based in Dubai. Art has followed me since childhood, growing from a habit into the way I make sense of colour, light and everything I notice around me.

I work mainly in acrylic, watercolor and oil, drawing from landscapes, quiet still life and the occasional strange idea that refuses to stay abstract. Every canvas starts as a feeling before it becomes a picture.

— Gulnaz Khafizova

Not what it seems

A quieter, stranger series — open to whatever the viewer brings to it.

No two viewers read these paintings the same way — that's deliberate. The subject shifts depending on how long you look, and what you look for.

Custom commissions

A portrait, a landscape from your own photos, or something more abstract — I work closely with you from the first sketch to the final coat of varnish.

  1. 1

    Share your idea

    Send reference photos, a mood, or just a rough sense of what you're picturing.

  2. 2

    I paint, you follow along

    Most commissions take 2–4 weeks depending on size and detail. I'll send progress photos as the piece develops.

  3. 3

    Final review and delivery

    Once you approve the finished piece, it's carefully packed and shipped to you.

A 50% deposit begins the work; the remaining balance is due on completion, before shipping.
